What can I do for free?

In version 1.1 you can import, arrange and trim recordings, check the joins, and listen to your full merge for free. A one-time Lifetime Unlock lets you export this merge and all future merges as M4A or WAV. There are no subscriptions or ads.

If you used version 1.0 and still have your free export, you can use it after upgrading. You can still play and share files you already exported.

How do I restore Lifetime Unlock?

In version 1.1, open Help & Privacy and tap Restore Purchases under Purchases. The same option is on the Lifetime Unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock.

Where do I add recordings?

In Apple Voice Memos, select one or more recordings, tap Share, and choose Audio Joiner in the system Share Sheet. Then open Audio Joiner and tap Import Shared Recordings.

Choose Add Recordings and select audio files through the iOS Files picker. Files stored in iCloud Drive or another provider may need to download before iOS passes them to Audio Joiner.

What format does Audio Joiner export?

Choose M4A (AAC) for a compact file or WAV (PCM) for uncompressed audio.

Does Audio Joiner change my original recordings?

No. Ordering and trimming are stored as project edits inside Audio Joiner. The original source files you selected are not modified.

Where is my exported file?

After export, use Share Result to save the audio file to Files or send it to another destination available in the iOS Share Sheet.

What happens when I close the Lifetime Unlock screen?

Your project stays available for editing and full preview. Files you already exported can still be played and shared from your export history.

Privacy or data-deletion request

Audio Joiner does not create an account or automatically send your project/audio data to the developer. If you previously emailed support and want the support information controlled by the developer deleted, contact xiangmucheng001@gmail.com.
